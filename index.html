🧪 Frequently asked questions

Everything parents and students need to know.

Still unsure about something? Send a WhatsApp enquiry and ask directly.

Where are lessons held?+

In-person lessons are held at Mathematics & Beyond, 214 Triq id-Duluri, Tal-Pietà, Malta. The exact room is confirmed with the class information.

Are lessons available online?+

Some sessions may be offered online depending on the class and arrangement. Send an enquiry to check the options currently available.

How many students are in each class?+

Classes are intentionally kept small—generally up to five students—so explanations, feedback and practice can remain focused.

Which years do you teach?+

Support is available for Year 9, Year 10 and Year 11 O Level chemistry students, including a From Scratch pathway for students rebuilding their foundations.

Are notes and worksheets included?+

Yes. Relevant notes, worksheets, guided practice and exam-style questions are provided as part of the learning programme.

How can parents enquire?+

Parents can call or send a WhatsApp message to +356 7963 7563. The enquiry form below prepares the WhatsApp message automatically.

How does the credit and payment system work?+

Lessons operate using prepaid credit. Families top up the student’s account, and the lesson cost is deducted after each session. Credit can be topped up through Revolut or by cash, as arranged.
