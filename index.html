The learning approach

Three steps.
One clear purpose.

Every lesson moves from understanding the idea to using it independently.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Break challenging chemistry into precise, manageable ideas connected to prior knowledge.

  2. 02

    Practise

    Build fluency through guided examples, purposeful classwork and immediate feedback.

  3. 03

    Apply

    Transfer knowledge to unfamiliar situations and communicate it using strong exam technique.
